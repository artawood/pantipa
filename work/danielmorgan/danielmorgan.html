DANIEL MORGAN

Video Production and Editing

Project Description

Daniel Morgan is a local stage actor in Pinole, California. I produced this short video for his video self portrait. My focus for this video is about Daniel transforming himself into a character on stage. Daniel uses one of the characters he plays named “Fagin”, a character from the musical Oliver. I used a song from that play named “Pick a pocket” which is also sung by the Fagin character.

Date Completed: December 2014
