I Notice

Motion Graphic Design

Project Description

"I Notice" was a poem written by Zora Rosenberg, a student from 826 Valencia, a non-profit organization in San Francisco, dedicated to helping children and young adults develop creative writing skills. In partnership with BBDO San Francisco, I worked with a group of students from the Academy of Art University to create motion graphics to visually convey the meaning of the poem.

Date Completed: March 2015
