The Light Experiment

Photography Project

Project Description

This purpose of this project was to experiment photography in dark room environment with limited lighting. I wanted to demonstrate that Backlight and Splitlight techniques can still be achieved even without a studio room or professional quality lightings and camera. I used Nikon D5000 as my camera, some ikea lamps, and took photos in my bedroom.

Date Completed: October 2014

Split Light

This image was taken with the Nikon D5000 at 26mm, ISO 400, with aperture at f 5, and shutter speed at 1/50 s. I pointed the light to the subject’s right side to produce split light effect and have the subject splash water on his face to imitate sweat to make the image more dramatic. The image was enhanced with added contrast using photoshop.

Back Light

This image was taken with Nikon D5000 at 32mm, ISO 400, with aperture at f 5.3 , and shutter speed at 1/80 s. I used myself as the subject and set the light behind me to produce backlight effect. Once I was in position, I took a photo of myself with a remote. The image was enhanced with added contrast using photoshop.
