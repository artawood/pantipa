ItsaCheckmate

Menu Management UI

ItsaCheckmate Integration

ItsaCheckmate was an early startup and their core product was integrating 3rd party delivery services like UberEats, DoorDash, and Grubhub with restuarant point of sales (POS) system. Guests would make the order online via the 3rd Delivery Platform, and the order is sent to ItsaCheckmate where the each menu item is validated with the POS menu. Once validated the order is directly injected into the POS.

I created this graphic here to help visualize the services the company can provide.

How does it work?

When I first join the company as their first designer, ItsaCheckmate did not have any customer facing UI. So I was task with design the company's first customer facing UI that help restaurant owners manage all their integrated third party delivery platform. The UI is called Menu Management. The UI allows restaurant operators to manage all their 3rd party delivery platform menu from a single location. Restaurant operators to mark an item sold out, and push that updates via API to all their integrated 3rd party platform.

User Persona

Before designing the UI, first I had to understand who were the users and what are their needs. I had the opportunity to collaborate with some Small-Medium Business customers, aka "mom and pop". I was able to get direct feedback about their needs and pain points in managing menus on different ordering platforms. From their feedback, I was able to construct user persona that was very important to me when I start on any design project related to the Menu Management UI.

Conceptualization

I did some brainstorming sessions, walking through the experience of a restaurant operator, and figuring out what would make their job more efficient when using the Menu Management UI. Then I map out the user flow to put together a journey that will help customers better manage their menus for all their integrated ordering platforms. One of the prominent feature is marking an item sold out. This feature is very critical to the customers because they don't need to go to each of their delivery service platform to mark an item sold out. They can just do it from one location in ItsaCheckmate Menu Management Portal.

Design

After validating the user flows, I move on to developing the medium fidelity wireframe, then proceeded to work on the high fidelity wireframe. Below is a hi-fi mockup of the feature for marking an item soldout.

Additional Features and Next Steps

The implementation of marking an item soldout became an instant success for the company, and many more features where added to the Menu Management UI. Because the company was growing at a phenomal pace, I became more focused on growing the design team. As a design leader, I established systems and structure for my expanding team to ensure efficiency and scale.
